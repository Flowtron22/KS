# 40K Combat Lab

A browser army builder and unit-versus-unit combat calculator. The previous battlefield, movement, objectives and AI battle simulation have been removed. Existing Army List saves remain compatible.

## Combat calculator

The starting example is **5 Custodian Guard vs 1 Cerastus Knight Lancer**. Choose factions and units on either side, or choose a unit/model count from the saved Army A/B. Select the defensive profile and one weapon profile per unit. Saved army wargear, attachments, Enhancements and abilities are not applied to combat automatically.

Select Shooting or Fight phase, choose who attacks first, and optionally let surviving models fight back in melee. Configure model counts, Toughness, Save, Invulnerable Save, Wounds per model, wounds remaining on the first model, weapon bearers, A, BS/WS, S, AP and D. Profiles can be overridden for a controlled experiment. Model counts are experimental and are not restricted to legal squad sizes. Every model shares the chosen defensive profile.

Supported effects: hit/wound modifiers capped at +/-1, re-roll 1s or failed hit/wound rolls, Benefit of Cover in shooting, Feel No Pain, Lethal Hits, Sustained Hits, critical-hit thresholds, BS/WS changes, Strength/attacks/AP/damage bonuses, defensive hit/wound modifiers, AP reduction and damage reduction (minimum 1 before Feel No Pain). A and D accept integers, D3, D6, and expressions such as 2D6+1. Unsupported expressions fail validation; Torrent profiles use automatic hits when supported weapon keywords and conditions are enabled; otherwise choose a custom profile. One weapon profile is resolved per unit; alternative strike/sweep profiles are separate choices. Named Custodes options use the September 2026 Codex preview: Rendax, Dacatarai, Kaptaris and Salvus check phase, target type, eligibility and readiness. Captain-General checks the receiving unit’s Custodes keyword and excludes Monsters/Vehicles; the range checkbox is a user assumption. Current Guard Impenetrable Defence assumes either unit is within objective range and excludes Monster/Vehicle targets. Repeated re-rolls do not stack; Sustained Hits uses the highest selected value. Conservai/Calistus are outside this attack calculation. See the faction-rule library below for additional selected effects and omitted mechanics.

**Roll attacks** displays attack-count dice when needed, Hit rolls, Wound rolls, Saving throws, Damage, and Feel No Pain rolls. Re-rolled dice show an arrow; hovering reveals the original result. The display limits each stage to 60 dice; JSON exports retain the complete trace. Excess normal damage does not spill to another model. Wounded models take damage first. Attacks back use surviving model counts, with selected weapon bearers assumed to survive first. Range, eligibility and fight order are supplied by the experiment rather than calculated from a battlefield.

**Run simulations** repeats the same scenario 1,000, 10,000 or 50,000 times in a web worker. Results show wounds lost and models destroyed on each side, chance of unit destruction, a 95% Wilson interval, and the wounds-lost distribution. Lost wounds are capped at remaining health; they are not uncapped raw damage. Every trial resets both units. Monte Carlo uncertainty does not account for omitted rules.

The dashboard places the dice table and result summary above compact unit cards. Defence, weapon stats, abilities, calculation details and exports expand on demand. A single roll shows actual losses and faded destroyed tokens; repeated simulations feature the target destruction chance and average losses.

**Roll attacks** generates a fresh seed by default. Open **Roll settings** and enable **Replay this seed** to repeat the displayed seed. The same seed reproduces a sequence or simulation batch. **New seed** generates another seed. Save/load one scenario locally or export/import scenario JSON. Export rolls and simulation results with the scenario and seed. Combat scenarios use `combat-lab-1`; legacy battlefield saves cannot be imported. Nothing is written back into Army List models after combat.

## Faction abilities and weapon keywords

All 36 catalogue selections have a source-linked rule library, including Marine chapters and Titan auxiliaries. By default, the picker lists the chosen unit’s abilities and army rules. Switch to all catalogue entries to find auras, Leader effects, Detachment abilities, Enhancements and other source profiles; this also includes imported allied entries. Search by rule name. The imported catalogue is not a promise that every official rule exists in the source or has an engine implementation.

Choose a rule, inspect its listed effects and source conditions, disable components that do not apply, then confirm conditions and availability. Only checked, supported components enter the dice sequence and worker simulations. The engine does not enforce all eligibility, geometry, resources, timing, army legality or bearer composition. A rule’s inclusion is not full implementation of its text. Community abilities have not all been verified against current Codexes. Older Guard abilities cannot be combined with the verified September Guard profile.

Explicit army choices include Oath of Moment, Dark Pacts, Doctrina alternatives, Chaos Knight Dread effects, riled-up Orks, Death Guard contagion components, T’au Guided/Markerlight effects and Votann Yield effects. Quality, blessing and ritual profiles are imported separately. Mutually exclusive choices are rejected where explicitly mapped. One-hit/one-wound re-roll effects use the first eligible failed roll once per attack sequence. Effects are merged without mutating base characteristics.

Weapon keywords supported here: Torrent, Twin-linked, Lethal Hits, fixed-number Sustained Hits, Devastating Wounds, Anti-Infantry/Monster/Vehicle, Ignores Cover, Blast/Blast X, Rapid Fire X, Melta X, Heavy and Lance. Supply half-range, Heavy eligibility (11e: unengaged, not set up this turn, moved no more than 3 inches), and charge conditions. Stealth grants shooting cover in this snapshot. Devastating Wounds bypass saves, resolve after normal wounds, receive Feel No Pain and do not spill between models. Lethal Hits always auto-wound and cannot generate a critical wound; no optional critical-fishing strategy is modelled.

Reference-only entries are visible and linked, never treated as implemented zero-effect selections. Movement, objectives, regeneration, dice-pool generation/substitution, prerequisite tests, self-damage costs, direct mortal-wound abilities, fight-on-death, Hazardous, Precision, Cleave, mixed/conditional weapon rules, and unrecognised keywords still require further implementation or manual scenario assumptions. The source is incomplete in places: for example, Astra Militarum officer Order permissions are present, but the full Voice of Command order effects are absent. Generic manual controls can model known numerical bonuses, but do not silently activate these rules.

Faction rule JSON is lazy-loaded from public/rules. Regenerate it with node tools/import-combat-rules.mjs after obtaining the pinned raw cache with the army importer, or pass an existing cache directory as the first argument. The importer checks the root-catalogue hashes, resolves linked ability/profile identifiers and retains numerical facts and source IDs rather than publishing rule prose. Conservative extraction produces selected combat components, not a general rules-language interpreter. Scenarios retain rule IDs, checked components, keyword conditions and rulesVersion metadata; importing rebuilds effects from the current library and rejects unavailable IDs or unsupported operators.

## Army Lists

Faction > Points > Detachments > Units > Review. The snapshot includes 34 main faction/chapter catalogues and two Titan auxiliary catalogues. Shared libraries/root imports are resolved. Allied, Legends and narrative entries are filtered by default. Configure model counts, Warlord, Leader/Support attachments, Enhancement selections, and equipment choices for individual models. Fixed weapons and replacement groups are preserved from the source catalogue; single-choice groups use dropdowns and bundled alternatives add their paired weapons together. New units receive source defaults. Legacy manual loadouts require an explicit Configure equipment action. Squad and equipment constraints with dynamic modifiers are flagged for source review; this is still partial validation. Build two independent browser-local armies and export/import JSON.

Army validation is partial: points budget, simple model-count prices, source model ranges, basic duplicate/Epic Hero limits, Warlord count, DP budget, Force Dispositions and attachment existence/counts. Complex conditional costs are flagged; unknown costs remain unknown. Static equipment selections and quantities are checked; dynamic replacement restrictions, model-composition conditions, specific Bodyguard eligibility, allied restrictions and full Enhancement legality remain partial. A passed basic check does not establish matched-play legality.

Use **Review > Share army image** to preview and save a PNG with the faction, points budget, Detachments/DP, Force Disposition, units, Warlord, Enhancements, attachments, equipment and notes. Supported devices can open native file sharing; other devices download the image. Long lists paginate and unresolved army checks remain visible in the export. Images are generated locally without uploading army data.

## Data and deployment

Faction JSON is in `public/armies/`, pinned to BSData commit `cc58ee3b2c23f90af9d00e7bf234cd703b762299` (9 October 2026). Numeric characteristics and selection facts are community data and have not been reconciled with the official points manual. Ability/rule prose is omitted. Regenerate with `node tools/import-armies.mjs`, using the local research cache or pinned public source. This is not a live or fully validated rules feed. The combat calculator overlays Custodian Guard Sodality defensive and Guardian Spear characteristics from the [official 24 September datasheet preview](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/2xxurx9j/first-look-adeptus-custodes-datasheets/). Other Custodes profiles and Army Lists still use the older community snapshot. Ka’tahs are based on the [25 September army rules preview](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/vygelghy/new-army-rules-from-codex-adeptus-custodes/). Saved older scenarios retain their characteristics until explicitly upgraded.

Production: https://codex-imperium.vercel.app. Vercel builds from the repository's `main` branch with root `solo-lab`, using `npm ci`, `npm run build` and output `dist`. No backend or environment variables are needed. Browser-local saves do not transfer automatically between domains.

## Development and verification

See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) for module boundaries and rules for extending the calculator.

Run `npm ci`, `npm run dev`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`, and `npm run build`. Tests cover faction data/army validation and attack calculations: Strength thresholds, AP/cover/invulnerable saves, damage allocation, Feel No Pain, re-rolls, dice expressions, surviving attacks back, deterministic seeds and agreement with an analytic damage expectation. Browser checks cover profile selection, phase/order changes, worker simulations, saved army handoff, scenario persistence, validation and responsive layout.
